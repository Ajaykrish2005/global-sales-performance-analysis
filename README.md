# Global Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Business Problem
The executive team required a comprehensive analysis of global sales data to identify top-performing markets, isolate categories operating at a loss, and optimize shipping operations. Additionally, raw datasets required extensive standardization to reduce reporting errors by 20-30%.

## Methodology & Tools
* **Data Cleaning:** Processed raw sales data into a structured format (`Cleaned Dataset.csv`).
* **SQL:** Executed advanced queries to aggregate regional sales, calculate average category profit, and track monthly sales trends (`SQL QUERIES.pdf` / `queries.sql`)[cite: 6].
* **Excel:** Conducted exploratory data analysis and formatted stakeholder-facing reports (`Excel Analysis.xlsx`).
* **Power BI:** Built interactive visualizations to clearly track KPIs and performance trends (`E Commerce Power BI Dashboard.pbix`).

## Key Business Insights
1. **Market Dominance:** The APAC (Asia Pacific) market generates the highest total revenue, closely followed by the EU[cite: 4, 5]. 
2. **Profitability Drain:** Despite strong overall revenue, the "Tables" sub-category is consistently generating negative profit[cite: 4, 5].
3. **Shipping Optimization:** "Standard Class" is the overwhelmingly dominant shipping method[cite: 4, 5].

## Strategic Recommendations
* **Allocate Budget to APAC:** Capitalize on the strong momentum in the Asia Pacific market[cite: 4, 5].
* **Audit the Furniture Category:** Immediately investigate shipping costs and supplier pricing for "Tables" to stop financial bleeding, or consider discontinuing low-margin models[cite: 4, 5].
* **Negotiate Carrier Rates:** Leverage the high volume of "Standard Class" shipments to negotiate bulk discounts with primary shipping carriers[cite: 4, 5].
